# Block Lo-Fi instrument samples

These sparse instrument samples come from
[`nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments`](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments)
and are licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Attribution: the tonejs-instruments contributors and the original sample authors
catalogued in its `sample-source-info.txt`, including Karoryfer, Versilian
Studios/VSO2, the University of Iowa, and named Freesound contributors.

Only a few notes from each instrument are bundled. Tone.js pitch-shifts the
nearest sample at playback time to keep the PWA download small.
