    Choose a board

    Enter the geohash shared by your group or workplace: 3 to 9 characters. A longer code covers a smaller area, and 9 is precise enough for a building. A place that straddles two or three cells can be written as a list, u0qj7z0y,u0qj7z0z, as long as those cells touch.

    Exact shows this geohash only. Each deeper level also includes notes from more precise geohashes inside it. For example, one level on u24 includes child boards such as u24j.

    Share this board

    Whoever you send this to sees the board and its notes before they open it. The link opens the live board.

    Choose a geohash

        Private key

        Not recommended. Your private key stays only in this open page's memory. It is not saved and is forgotten when you reload or close the page. Use Amber, a browser extension, or a bunker instead.

        A new one-time key is created for one note. It remains available for up to 24 hours so you can finish publishing. Leaving anonymous mode deletes it. It cannot buy a weekly or yearly subscription.

        Finish signing in with Amber

        Amber has it on your clipboard. Tap the button to hand it over.

        Connect Amber to this board
