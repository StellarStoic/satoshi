    Every note and image you pin is stored on wss://nostr.satoshi.si by default, because that is the relay this board reads.       Board levels

        Choose a board

        Enter the geohash shared by your group or workplace: 3 to 9 characters. A longer code covers a smaller area, and 9 is precise enough for a building. A place that straddles two or three cells can be written as a list, u0qj7z0y,u0qj7z0z, as long as those cells touch.

        Exact shows this geohash only. Each deeper level also includes notes from more precise geohashes inside it. For example, one level on u24 includes child boards such as u24j.

        A kept place already decides this. Unlock it in “Show notes around me”.

            Log in to Nostr

            Your signer proves the note is yours. Satoshi.si never needs your private key.

            Opens Telegram to confirm who you are, then creates your Nostr identity. No password or seed phrase.

            Private key

            Not recommended. Your private key stays only in this open page's memory. It is not saved and is forgotten when you reload or close the page. Use Amber, a browser extension, or a bunker instead.

            A new one-time key is created for one note. It remains available for up to 24 hours so you can finish publishing. Leaving anonymous mode deletes it. It cannot buy a weekly or yearly subscription.

            Finish signing in with Amber

            Amber has it on your clipboard. Tap the button to hand it over.

            Connect Amber to this board

            Amber and this board then talk over a relay, so no key reaches this page and nothing is copied by hand.

            The link behind the code

            In Amber, tap Add a new application, then Paste from clipboard (or Scan a QR Code). That is this invitation link, pasted once — not a signature.

            Your Nostr ID is ready

            Pinstr generated a Nostr identity for your Telegram account.

            To export its private key:
            Top-right person icon → Nostr account → Your key → Export my key.

            You can export it later while Pinstr keeps its encrypted copy. Choosing “Remove our copy” makes your exported key your only recovery.
